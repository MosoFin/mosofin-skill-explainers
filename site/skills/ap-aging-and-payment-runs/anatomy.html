The structure of the AP aging and payment runs skill, and what it does

On main the skill is one 911-line file. The rewrite described here turns it into a 131-line card and seven reference files, and it keeps the original text. Two things set this skill apart. It never reads a bank balance and calls it available cash. And the payment run is only a proposal: the skill sends, releases and schedules no payment. This page shows how the files connect, the run stage by stage, and the source behind each step. It also shows the five audit findings, four fixed and one open, and one observation.

Observation

The discount formula is the simpler one

The skill's formula is Discount % × 365 / (Net − Discount days). For 2/10 Net 30 it gives 36.5% a year. The fuller textbook formula also divides by the discounted price and uses a 360-day year, and gives 36.73% [1]. The skill says "~36.5%" and "few uses of cash return 36% a year". So the simpler formula does not change a decision. The audit did not change it, and records it for the skill owner.
