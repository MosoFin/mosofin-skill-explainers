The structure of the recurring transaction builder skill, and what it does

On main the skill is a 164-line card and six reference files, 989 lines in all. The split commit f476f57 made the card on 25 October 2026, and pull request #1 merged it on 26 October 2026. The bookkeeping-audit commit 46708f0 changed the skill on 27 October 2026. The skill designs, validates and audits a recurring schedule. It never creates the template, posts an entry or schedules anything. A person enters the specification. Then the skill audits each period for a posting. This page shows how the files connect, the run stage by stage, the tools and sources behind each step, and the audit.

The audit checked the five sources on 28 Oct 2026 through search results. Nobody opened the pages. All five are US sources. Other countries have their own lease standards, holiday calendars and price indexes. Some items have no outside authority behind them. They are the six stages, the Pending status, the NOT POSTED header and the six sheets. They are also the 12-month or 24-month horizon, the duplicate check and the run audit. The worked example uses a made-up company, Northbrook Trading, and made-up figures. Ask your accountant what applies to you.
