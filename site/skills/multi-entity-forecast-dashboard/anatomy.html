A3. Cash is the cumulative net increase, which is right only from a zero opening

The template sets cash to the cumulative sum of the monthly net increase (cashSeries, opening). forecast-method.md says the same. That sum equals cash only if cash was zero at the start of the range. mosofin-workflow.md states the zero-opening condition for A/R, A/P and inventory, but not for cash. The template has no field for opening cash. In the example, the label "Cash at end of Sep" sits on $129,100, the Q3 increase, and not on a bank balance.

A6. "Intercompany removed" removes one side only

The row subtracts arIc from receivables and keeps the matching payable in A/P. The data object has no apIc field. In the example the row gives 67,300 − 57,000 = 10,300. Removal of both sides gives 67,300 − 47,500 = 19,800, the same as before. So the row is not a consolidation elimination [2] [3].

assets/dashboard-template.html · renderAccounts · open
Medium · open

A7. Upside can fall below base, and the P10 and P90 names suggest percentiles

Upside is the mean of the last two months of operating cash flow. When the last month is the best, upside is less than base, and P90 = max(upside, rate) equals base. In the example, upside is 48,850 and base is 52,100. The names P10 and P90 are presets, not percentiles.
