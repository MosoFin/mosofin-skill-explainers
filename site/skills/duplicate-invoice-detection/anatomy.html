The structure of the duplicate invoice detection, and what it does

The skill was one 866-line file. The card format is now on main of MosoFin/mosofin-finance-skills, merged in PR #1 on 26 October 2026: a 154-line card and seven reference files. A bookkeeping-audit fix followed on 27 October 2026. Nine of the ten rules run as queries on the connected books, because the workspace holds the whole AP register and the vendor master. The tenth rule, two vendor records that share one bank account, is the fraud test. Mosofin does not expose bank details, so on that path the output reports Rule 7 as not run, with an owner. On the upload path, uploaded payee bank details let Rule 7 run. This page shows how the files connect, the run step by step, the source behind each step, and the findings.

Medium · fixed

The default sensitivity hid matches, and the exposure counted the valid bill

Before the audit, Standard flagged High and above, and the text said nothing about the other matches. The exposure was the total in flagged duplicates, so the total could include the valid original. Now every rule runs and the skill lists every match. The sensitivity decides only the headline exposure and the recovery plan, and the Summary counts the matches below it. Exposure is the cluster total less the one record treated as valid. The same commit adds near-match invoice numbers and manual journal entries in the read. It adds Rule 7 from uploaded payee details, closed-period corrections with draft entries and reviewer disposition columns. It also asks for the Rule 8 threshold and the scan window.
