How duplicate invoice detection is built, and what it does

A 154-line card with seven reference files behind it, split on 25 Oct 2026 from an 866-line SKILL.md. The skill scans the whole accounts payable population with ten rules for bills entered or paid twice, puts unpaid duplicates first, and refuses a false "clean": a partial scan, or a rule that did not run, never reads as "no duplicates found". This page shows where the weight sits, the run step by step, and what the 27 Oct audit found and how each finding was handled.

Nine findings: seven fixed, two partly fixed on 27 Oct

An independent senior-bookkeeper review of the card and every reference file. Verdict: fit with fixes. The finding that mattered most was that the default sensitivity silently left out most of what the card promised.

High · fixed

H1 — The default sensitivity switched off half the rules, and Rule 10 never flagged at all

"Standard (default): flag any rule match with High+ confidence." With Rules 3 and 4 at Medium, 5 at Medium-Low, 8 at Low and 10 "Low for duplicate", a default run flagged only Rules 1, 2, 6 and 9, and even Strict never reached 8 and 10. A user who accepted the defaults got a clean report with vendor-name variants, near-date duplicates and unapplied credits unreported.

Fix. Flagging is split from reporting: every rule always runs and every match is listed on Flagged Pairs with its confidence; sensitivity decides only the headline exposure and the recovery plan. Rule 10 sits outside the scale — it is money owed, not a duplicate suspicion. The Summary states the matches below the threshold, by count and amount.

Basis: the skill's own rule against a false "clean"; standard AP duplicate-review practice reports every match for a reviewer to clear.

High · fixed

H2 — Exposure counted the legitimate original as well as the duplicate

Exposure was "the total amount in flagged duplicates", and the only per-cluster figure was the cluster total. In a two-record cluster one bill is real, so the summary reported twice the money at risk and an overstated claim against the vendor.

Fix. Defined once in Step 5: duplicate exposure = cluster total − the one record treated as valid (for paid clusters, total paid − invoice total). Sheet 2 gains a Duplicate Exposure column; the cluster total stays for reference only.

Basis: standard practice in measuring a duplicate payment: only the excess over the valid invoice is recoverable.

Medium · fixed

M1 — No rule caught "an invoice number altered by a character"

The card promised it, but Rule 1 needed identical normalized numbers and Rules 2–3 an identical amount within 0 or 7 days. "INV-01043" against "1043A" three weeks later passed every rule.

Fix. Normalization now strips leading zeros and common prefixes and suffixes, keeping the raw number; Rule 1 adds a near-match test — same vendor, same amount, numbers one character apart or one contained in the other, any date in the window — at High confidence. Still ten rules.

Basis: near-match invoice numbers are a core test in standard AP duplicate audits.

method.md Steps 1–2
Medium · fixed

M2 — The upload path did not ask for payments made outside the bill process

The classic duplicate is a bill paid through bill pay and again by a hand-written cheque or card charge entered straight to expense. The Mosofin path reads those direct purchases; the upload checklist asked only for the AP register, which leaves them out.

Fix. A required upload row for cheques, expenses and card charges by vendor over the same window; search_journal_entries added to the Step 0 batch.

Basis: the house rule that both paths run the same checks; Rule 9 (two channels) depends on this population.

Medium · fixed

M3 — "Void or remove" ignored closed periods, and no correcting entry was proposed

"For open (unpaid) duplicates, simply void or remove the duplicate." Voiding a bill in a closed period changes reported expense and payables; for a paid duplicate, no entry was offered at all.

Fix. Unpaid, period open: propose voiding, for a person to do. Unpaid, period closed: a vendor credit dated in the current open period, Dr Accounts Payable / Cr the original account. Paid: Dr vendor receivable / Cr the original expense account, in the current period, cleared on credit or refund.

Basis: standard close practice of correcting in the current period rather than altering a closed one; ASC 250 for anything material.

method.md Step 5 · SKILL.md Stage 5
Medium · fixed

M4 — Rule 7 was refused even when the user could upload bank details

The limit belongs to the Mosofin surface, but the card said Rule 7 "cannot run here" on every path, though a bookkeeper can export a payee list from the bank or payment platform.

Fix. Rule 7 runs on the upload path from user-supplied payee details (last four digits plus routing or sort code), compared in the conversation, masked in output, never stored. The card now says it cannot run "from the Mosofin connection".

Basis: the house rule against refusing work the skill can reasonably do; the same-bank-account test is a standard AP fraud check.

Medium · fixed

M5 — No column for the reviewer's decision, though the next run depends on it

Step 7 persists "cleared clusters with reason and date", but Sheet 2 had no Disposition, Reviewer, Date or Reason columns, so the list had nowhere to come from.

Fix. Sheet 2 gains Disposition (Confirmed duplicate / Cleared), Reason, Reviewed by and Date; Step 7 saves only rows marked Cleared.

Basis: standard review evidence: a cleared exception records who cleared it, when and why.

Low · partly

Two low findings, both partly fixed

  • L1 · Rule 8's threshold never defined. The round-number cluster threshold is now asked for and stated on the Summary; without one, clusters are listed for review. Partly fixed: the audit's suggested default (3 bills within 30 days) was not adopted, because the house brief forbids hard-coding a threshold.
  • L2 · "Same period" and "scan wider" not quantified. Rule 4's window now equals the Rule 3 window, and intake C asks how far back to scan, with the window read stated under "Parameters used". Partly fixed: no fixed 12-month default, because the card forbids defaulting the period.
