The structure of the duplicate invoice detection, and what it does

On main the skill is one 866-line file. The rewrite described here turns it into a 135-line card and seven reference files, and it keeps the original text. Two things set this skill apart. Nine of its ten rules run as queries on the connected books, because the workspace holds the whole AP register and the vendor master. The tenth rule, two vendor records that share one bank account, is the fraud test, and Mosofin does not expose bank details. So every output reports Rule 7 as not run, with an owner. This page shows how the files connect, the run step by step, and the source behind each step. It also shows the two audit findings: one fixed in the rewrite, one open.
