How the closing entries and trial balance skill is built, and what it does

A 166-line card with seven reference files behind it, split on 25 Oct 2026 from a 1,013-line file. At year end it checks the pre-closing trial balance, works out the closing entries account by account, proves the post-closing trial balance and ties retained earnings to the balance sheet — after first testing whether the platform has already closed the year, to stop a double close. The audit on 27 Oct found that on auto-closing platforms the draws and distributions close was never proposed, member income was closed to the wrong account, and the OCI entry had its sides reversed; all nine findings are fixed on the branch.

Audit → findings

Nine findings, all fixed on 27 Oct, with the standard behind each

An independent senior-bookkeeper audit on 27 Oct 2026 gave the verdict "fit with fixes". The one thing that mattered most: on QuickBooks Online and Xero, both auto-closing, "auto-closed → propose no entries" plus type-based classification meant owner draws, distributions and member capital allocations were never closed. Each finding was checked against the skill before acting; all were fixed in af87caf.

High · fixed

H1 — "Auto-closed → propose no entries" dropped the draws and distributions close

Owner's Draw, Partner Distributions and Dividends Paid are equity-typed on QBO and Xero, so type-only classification called them permanent and the platform's roll never touched them; in the auto-closed state the skill proposed nothing and draws grew year after year.

Fixed. Step 2 makes equity-typed draws, distributions and dividends an explicit exception, identified from the equity GL and detail type and confirmed with the user. In the auto-closed state no revenue or expense entries are proposed, but the draws/distributions entry and the partnership/LLC member allocation still are, unless already posted. The card rule and Stage 4 match.

Basis: Standard bookkeeping practice (draws closed to capital each year); IRS Form 1065 Schedules K-1 and M-2 for partnership allocation.

references/method.md · Step 0b, Step 2 · SKILL.md rules, Stage 4
High · fixed

H2 — Partnership and LLC income closed to Retained Earnings, with no ratio asked

Entries 1–2 credited "Retained Earnings" for every entity type, contradicting the edge case; the read-back promised capital kept per member with no input that could produce it.

Fixed. New intake D2 asks the profit-sharing ratio and whether capital is kept per member (required for those types, never equal by assumption); Step 5 gives the partnership/LLC variant to each member's capital, or via Income Summary, and sole proprietors close to Owner Capital. Gate 3 row added.

Basis: Partnership accounting per the operating agreement; IRS Form 1065 Schedule K-1.

High · fixed

H3 — The OCI entry had its sides reversed, and OCI was added into closing RE

DR OCI — [item] $X (if a loss) / CR AOCI doubles a debit-balance loss instead of zeroing it, and the tie-out added OCI into closing retained earnings, so the roll-forward missed the balance sheet by the OCI amount.

Fixed. Loss: DR AOCI / CR OCI; gain: DR OCI / CR AOCI. OCI removed from the closing RE formula and Sheet 5; AOCI gets its own roll-forward.

Basis: ASC 220 and IAS 1 presentation of other comprehensive income.

references/method.md · Steps 6, 8, Sheet 5
Medium · fixed

M1 — The upload path could not run the double-close test the card promised

No first-day TB or equity GL was asked for, so on uploads the closed state was the user's assertion — and the user who does not know their system auto-closes is the one who double-closes.

Fixed. The upload checklist requires the first-day new-year TB (or balance sheet) and the equity GL; Step 0b runs the same tests on them, and without them reports "user-asserted, not tested".

Basis: Standard year-end close practice.

SKILL.md upload checklist · references/method.md · Step 0b
Medium · fixed

M2 — RE tests did not match how QBO and Xero show retained earnings

Those platforms show prior-years RE plus a separate Net Income line at year end, so comparing RE at two year ends measures last year's result and the tie-out failed on correct files.

Fixed. Test 3 compares RE on the first day of the new year with RE + Net Income at year end; Step 8 ties closing RE to the first-day RE line; the upload row and gates row are reworded.

Basis: Standard practice for platforms that present current-year earnings separately.

references/method.md · Steps 0, 0b, 8 · SKILL.md · references/gates.md
Medium · fixed

M3 — Opening retained earnings was not agreed to last year's reported figures

The continuity figure was a live read, so a back-posting into a closed year was already inside it.

Fixed. Step 1 agrees opening RE or total capital to last year's final statements or tax return, [manual], and stops to ask on a difference; the document is Recommended on both paths.

Basis: Agreeing opening equity to the prior year's issued figures (corporate return Schedules L and M-2; partnership M-2).

references/method.md · Step 1 · SKILL.md · references/gates.md
Medium · fixed

M4 — The accounting basis was never asked

Cash and accrual trial balances give different net income and retained earnings, and the skill picked the basis on each read itself.

Fixed. New intake J, Required, passed as accounting_method on every read and stated in the Sheet 1 header.

Basis: Standard reporting practice: entries must tie to the statements and the return.
