The structure of the closing entries skill, and what it does

On main the skill is a 166-line card and seven reference files, 1,278 lines in all. The split commits made the card on 25 October 2026. Pull request #1 merged it on 26 October 2026. The bookkeeping-audit commit af87caf changed the card, gates.md and method.md on 27 October 2026. The skill tests if the year is already closed before it proposes an entry. It posts nothing and locks no period. This page shows how the files connect, the run stage by stage, the tools and sources behind each step, and the audit findings.

A4. overview.md says accrual basis only

overview.md says that the skill "applies the standard closing procedure for accrual-basis entities". Intake J accepts the cash basis and passes it on every read. The bookkeeping audit added J and did not change this line.

A5. The tie-outs speak only of retained earnings

Step 8 checks the "increase in RE from closing entries". Sheet 5 shows "Opening RE + Net Income − Dividends". For an LLC or a sole proprietor, the increase goes to member or owner capital, as Step 5 and the edge cases say. The worked example states the tie-outs on member capital.

references/method.md · Step 8 and Step 10 · open
Low · open

A6. The P&L cross-check cannot find a wrong type

On QuickBooks the P&L follows the account type. So the Step 4 cross-check passes when an account has the wrong type. In the example, "Customer Deposits" has the type Other Income and passes the check. Only the Step 2 type and name review finds it. One sentence in Step 4 can say this.
