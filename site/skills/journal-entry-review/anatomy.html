The structure of the journal entry review skill, and what it does

On main the skill is a 162-line card and seven reference files, 1,190 lines in total. The split, fad4441, merged in PR #1 on 26 October 2026. A bookkeeping-audit commit, 510b0ca, followed on 27 October 2026. The skill is a read-only screen. It reads every entry in the period and scores each entry against 21 risk indicators. Then it reviews the top entries, shows patterns first and lists every test that it could not run. It makes no entry and posts nothing. It never concludes that fraud occurred. A flag is a question for a person. This page shows how the files connect, the run stage by stage, the source behind each step and 11 open audit findings.

Sources

The five outside sources, numbered as on the learn page

  1. OpenStax: Principles of Accounting, Volume 1, 8.3 Describe Internal Controls within an Organization. Two people in most transactions, and custody kept apart from the records.
  2. U.S. Government Accountability Office: Standards for Internal Control in the Federal Government (the Green Book), GAO-25-107721. Segregation of duties, and why a smaller entity finds it harder. The Green Book applies to US federal entities. The pages use it as a widely cited statement of the control idea, not as a rule for Northbrook.
  3. ACFE: Occupational Fraud 2024: A Report to the Nations. Control weaknesses in fraud cases, and fewer checks in small organizations.
  4. PCAOB: AS 2401, Consideration of Fraud in a Financial Statement Audit. Tests of journal entries and other adjustments. It applies to audits of public companies only.
  5. PCAOB: Audit Focus: Journal Entries (staff publication). Completeness of the population, and selection characteristics. It applies to audits of public companies only.

The audit checked all five sources through search results on 28 Oct 2026. Nobody opened the pages. Sources 1, 2, 4 and 5 are US. Source 3 is international. Some items have no outside authority behind them. These are the 21 indicators, the verdict tags, NOT RUN and "Control not in place". The nine sheets and every example figure are also the skill's own or made up. The scoring weights, the period-end window and the just-below band are the worked example's choices. The skill never detects fraud. It finds entries that deserve a question. Check with your accountant or auditor what applies to you.
