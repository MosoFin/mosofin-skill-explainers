How the credit memo and refund handler is built, and what it does

A 153-line card with seven reference files behind it, split on 25 Oct 2026 from a 900-line file. It handles money going back the other way — customer credit memos and refunds, vendor credits and refunds, sales returns, chargebacks and goodwill credits: it names the type, validates each credit against the original invoice or bill, checks that the tax reversal mirrors the original, proposes the entry and lists unapplied credits in both directions. An independent bookkeeping audit on 27 Oct found ten problems, three of them entries or tests that gave wrong answers; all ten are fixed on the branch.

An independent senior-bookkeeper audit on 27 Oct 2026 gave the verdict "fit with fixes": the type, tax-mirroring and no-second-journal discipline is good, but goodwill credits were booked as bad debt, refunds of an existing credit hit revenue twice, and the over-credit test ran against a balance the credit had already reduced. High · fixed

H1 — A goodwill credit was booked as bad debt, and the entry did not balance without tax

Goodwill credit notes and bad-debt write-offs shared one entry, DR Bad Debt Expense (or Goodwill Allowance) $net / DR Tax Payable $tax (only if tax recoverable) / CR AR $gross. Drop the tax line and the debits fall short of the credit; "Goodwill Allowance" is not an account.

Fixed. Split into two. A goodwill credit note is DR Sales Returns & Allowances / DR Output Tax (mirrors the original) / CR AR. A write-off is DR Bad Debt Expense (or Allowance) gross / CR AR gross, with the tax reclassified only under claimed bad-debt relief, [manual]. "Goodwill Allowance" removed; intake E and the gates reason row reworded.

Basis: ASC 606 and IFRS 15 (a concession to a customer reduces the transaction price); ASC 326 for credit losses; VAT/GST credit-note and bad-debt relief rules.

High · fixed

H2 — A refund of a credit already on account reversed revenue and tax a second time

The single refund template, DR Sales Returns $net / DR Tax $tax / CR Cash $gross, is right only for a direct refund. When a credit memo was issued first, the refund of that balance must clear A/R; following the template reverses revenue and tax twice and leaves A/R in credit.

Fixed. Two forms: a refund of a credit or overpayment already on account is DR AR / CR Cash; the existing template stays for a direct refund with no prior credit memo. Before proposing a refund the skill checks for an existing credit memo or unapplied payment. The overpayment edge case now debits A/R (or Customer Deposits if reclassified).

Basis: Double-entry practice: a refund settles the receivable the credit memo created; standard bookkeeping practice.

High · fixed

H3 — The over-credit test read a balance the credit had already reduced

"Confirm the credit amount ≤ the invoice balance" compared a recorded credit with what was left after it, so it passed for the wrong reason, and a later price adjustment on a paid invoice showed as an unapproved over-credit.

Fixed. The test is now credits to date (this one plus earlier ones) ≤ the original invoice amount. The balance before the credit decides whether it is applied or becomes a refund or credit on account; neither is an exception. Matching wording in edge-cases.md, Sheet 5, overview.md, gates.md and the card.

Basis: Standard credit-control practice: total credits may not exceed the amount invoiced.

Medium · fixed

M1 — Pro-rata tax reversal was wrong on mixed-rate invoices

"For partial credits → reverse proportional tax" reclaims output tax that was never charged when the credited line was zero-rated or exempt.

Fixed. Partial credits reverse tax at the rate of the line(s) credited; pro-rata only for a whole-invoice percentage reduction, and said so.

Basis: VAT/GST credit-note rules: the adjustment follows the rate charged on the supply credited.

Medium · fixed

M2 — The unapplied-credit register used date-bounded searches and missed old credits

A search limited to the review period finds only credits dated in it; old unapplied credits are the ones that matter, so the "vendor credits not taken" figure was understated and presented as complete.

Fixed. The population is the A/R and A/P aging detail at period end, filtered to credit memos, vendor credits and unapplied payments; searches only enrich rows. Without aging detail the register is labelled "credits dated in [period] only".

Basis: Standard A/R and A/P aging practice: open items are listed as at a date, whatever their age.

Medium · fixed

M3 — Every chargeback was reversed to revenue at once

The template booked every chargeback as a revenue and tax reversal, whether still disputed (and possibly won back) or a fraud loss on a sale that did happen.

Fixed. Intake B asks disputed, accepted or fraud loss. Disputed: a chargeback receivable, no revenue reversal. Accepted dispute: the existing template. Fraud loss: a loss entry with the tax treatment [manual], as for a write-off.

Basis: Card-network chargeback and representment process; ASC 606 variable consideration; bad-debt relief rules for the fraud case.

Medium · fixed

M4 — Credits issued after period end for sales before it were not considered for accrual

The edge case said to "post to the credit's period unless materiality forces a prior-period adjustment" — mixing a cut-off accrual with error correction.

Fixed. Post at the credit's own date; for an earlier period still open, propose a refund-liability accrual for the user to decide; restate a prior period only to correct an error.

Basis: ASC 606 and IFRS 15 refund liability; ASC 855 subsequent events; ASC 250 and IAS 8 for prior-period errors.

Medium · fixed

M5 — Unapplied vendor credits were called money owed without checking with the vendor

"These are money you are owed and have not taken" — many are duplicate entries, credits already refunded, or for vendors no longer used, which the books alone cannot show.

Fixed. Reworded to credits recorded and not yet used, to be confirmed with the supplier's statement before claiming; "Confirm with vendor statement" is the first action on Sheet 4 for credits older than one billing cycle. overview.md qualified.

Basis: Standard accounts-payable practice: agree vendor credits to the supplier statement before claiming them.
