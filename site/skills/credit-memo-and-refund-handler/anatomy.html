The structure of the credit memo and refund handler skill, and what it does

The skill was one 900-line file. On main it is now a 153-line card and seven reference files. Commit 34adf3c made the split on 25 October 2026, and pull request #1 merged it on 26 October 2026. A bookkeeping-audit commit, 900f739, then changed the method on 27 October 2026. Two things set this skill apart. The reason for a credit decides the entry, and no system holds the reason. And the skill writes nothing: every entry is a draft that a person posts. This page shows how the files connect, the run stage by stage, and the source behind each step. It also shows the audit: four fixes on main, one check that main passes, and nine open findings.

The audit checked the seven sources on 28 Oct 2026 through search results. Nobody opened the pages. The worked example is a US business, and US states set sales tax. So the pages cite no US tax authority for the tax lines. Sources 3 to 5 show the same idea in the UK and Australia. Some items have no outside authority behind them. They are the eight credit types as a list, the four Step 2 checks and the six sheets. The vendor-first order, the 7.5% rate and every example figure have none either. The worked example uses made-up figures. Ask your accountant and your tax authority what applies to you.
