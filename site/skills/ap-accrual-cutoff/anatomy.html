The structure of the AP accrual cutoff skill, and what it does

The skill was one 903-line file. On main it is now a 161-line card and seven reference files. Pull request #1 merged them on 26 October 2026. A bookkeeping-audit commit, 6815813, then changed the method on 27 October 2026. The skill finds three groups of costs with no bill in the books. They are goods received and not invoiced (GRNI), late bills for work done in the month, and recurring vendors with no bill. It puts each cost in one group only. It drafts each accrual and its reversing entry, and it posts nothing. This page shows how the files connect, the run stage by stage, and the source behind each step. It also shows the audit: three changes fixed on main, one check that main passes, and eleven open findings for the skill owner.

The audit checked the six sources on 28 Oct 2026 through search results. Nobody opened the pages. All six are US sources. Sources 3 and 4 are audit standards for public companies, and the pages cite them as background only. Publication 538 is about tax, and book timing can differ from tax timing. Some items have no outside authority behind them. They are the seven intake questions, the three groups and their order, the seven sheets and the verdict tags. The $500 threshold, the 1–10 October window, the aggregate test in the example and every example figure have none either. The public pages present these items as the skill's rules or as the example's choices. Ask your accountant what applies to you.
