The two sides
Your limits

Enter each item as the change it makes to the GL balance. A negative amount makes the GL balance smaller.

DescriptionTypeAmount, change to GLDate opened

The page counts the age in calendar days: the as-of date minus the date opened. An item is over 3 months when the as-of date is later than the same day three calendar months after the date opened. The skill does not say if it counts days or calendar months, so that choice is this page's. A residual at or below the write-off limit, with no investigating items, gets a proposed write-off to a P&L account. Every entry is a proposal for a person to post. A status of Reconciled with proposed entries rests on a person posting them.

Using it

What it asks, needs and gives back

Asks firstUse MosoFin for this run, or upload the data yourself? Which company or entities? Which accounts? Which period end? Where is the support for each account outside the books? What materiality threshold applies? Do you have last period's reconciliation? Who prepares and who reviews? On the MosoFin path, it also asks for the approved write-off limit and its P&L account.
NeedsThe skill needs the trial balance at period end, the support for each account in scope and the chart of accounts. The GL detail and last period's reconciliation make the work stronger. You type in the accounts, the period end, the company name and the base currency.
Gives backThe skill gives back one sheet for each account, with the two sides, the items, a roll-forward, proposed entries and a carry-forward check. A coverage sheet shows how the skill got each side. It asks before it makes an Excel file.
NeverThe skill never reads your data before you answer its questions and say yes to its read-back. It never writes to your books, plugs a difference or corrects a timing item. It never calls an account reconciled without independent support, and never chooses the period, the threshold or the company.
