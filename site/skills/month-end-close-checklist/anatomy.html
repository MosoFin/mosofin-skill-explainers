The structure of the month-end close checklist skill, and what it does

On main the skill is one 991-line file. The rewrite described here turns it into a 127-line card and seven reference files, and it keeps the original text. Two things set this skill apart. It is the hub of the close: its task list names 15 other skills in the library. And it observes and reports only: it locks no period, posts no entry and marks no task complete. This page shows how the files connect, the run stage by stage, and the source behind each step. It also shows the three audit findings, all fixed, and one observation.

Medium · fixed

Twenty names of skills that are not in the library

F2. The task list and its notes named 20 skills that are not in the library, in 24 places. Examples are year-end-close, intercompany-reconciliation and ipo-readiness-accounting. Where the task name already said what the work is, the commit removed the bracketed name. Each "see" sentence now describes the work. F3. The card's read-back example said "Friday 7 November". In 2026, 7 November is a Saturday. The example now says "Friday 6 November".

"The four inventory tasks", but the list has three

Step 2's example says that the scoping step removes "the four inventory tasks". The Step 1 list has three tasks with "inventory" in the name. It records the difference for the skill owner.
