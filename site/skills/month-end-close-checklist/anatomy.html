Ten findings, all fixed on 27 Oct, with the practice behind each

An independent senior-bookkeeper audit on 27 Oct 2026 gave the verdict "fit with fixes". The one thing that mattered most: the inter-period continuity check, billed as the most valuable tie-out item, compared two live reads of the same ledger. Each finding was checked against the skill before acting; all were fixed in 937f77b.

High · fixed

H1 — The continuity check compared the ledger with itself, so it could never find a reopened period

The prior-period TB was pulled live (or exported today) and compared with this period's opening balances. A back-posting into July is already in both, so they always agree — yet the text said a break "means a prior period was reopened".

Fixed. Continuity compares last month's TB as reported (user-supplied, [manual]) with a fresh read of the same date, per account. Without it the check is "not performed — no reported prior TB", never a pass; a P&L and retained-earnings break in the first month of a fiscal year is noted as expected. Balances are still never persisted: the user supplies the reported TB each month.

Basis: Agreeing opening balances to the signed-off prior-period figures, as in AU-C 510 and ISA 510.

M1 — [gated] meant two different things

Gate 2 defines [gated] as an approval-gated tool; method.md used it on whole task groups to mean "partly observable, partly manual", so the coverage sheet could not say which.

Fixed. [gated] is kept for the Gate 2 meaning only; mixed groups are tagged "mixed — see each task" with per-part verdicts, and the coverage sheet has one row per part.

Basis: The skill's own Gate 2 definition; a coverage record must be unambiguous.

Medium · fixed

M2 — How an entry proves a task was never defined on a first run

Only the saved Step 9 preference said which account or entry proves each [auto] task, so a first run would match by memo text or guess.

Fixed. Step 3 states each [auto] task's test before reading — accounts, side, date window, compared with last month — and shows it in the Evidence column; with no account in the chart the task is [manual].

Basis: Standard close-review practice: evidence is defined before it is gathered.

M3 — Standard small-business close checks were missing

No task cleared Undeposited Funds, Uncategorized or Ask My Accountant, reviewed Opening Balance Equity, or checked that last month's accruals were reversed.

Fixed. Day 3: prior-month accruals reversed, [auto]. Day 4: clearing and suspense accounts at nil or listed with an owner, [auto].

Basis: Standard bookkeeping practice for QuickBooks and Xero files.

M4 — The tie-out claimed to see whether a reconciliation was recorded

"Flag any [account] without a reconciliation recorded" — no listed tool reads reconciliation status.

Fixed. Reconciliation status is [manual], from the owner or the workpaper, agreed to the ledger; Sheet 6 adds reconciled balance per workpaper, ledger balance and difference.

Basis: Standard practice: a reconciliation is evidenced by its workpaper, not by the ledger.

M5 — The accounting basis was never asked

"The accounting method on the reads" cannot reveal the basis, because the skill chooses it; yet basis decides whether a third of the tasks apply.

Fixed. New intake J, cash or accrual, Required; passed as accounting_method on every read and stated in the Sheet 1 header; accrual balances only corroborate.

Basis: Standard reporting practice.

Medium · fixed

M6 — "Measured" close timing used the transaction date

Close entries are dated the last day of the month whatever day they were entered, so the lessons-learned sheet would report a zero-slip close every month.

Fixed. Step 7 uses the entry-created timestamp; if the read returns none, the measurement is [manual] and said so.

Basis: Standard close-metrics practice.

M7 — No review or approval evidence per task

Tasks had an owner and status but no reviewer or review date, and Sheet 6 had a single sign-off.

Fixed. Reviewer (role) and Reviewed on columns, [manual]; Sheet 6 sign-off is Prepared by / date — Reviewed by / date.
