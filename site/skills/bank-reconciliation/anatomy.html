Low · fixed

A sandbox-only path for the spreadsheet helper

Outputs says "If creating xlsx, read first: /mnt/skills/public/xlsx/SKILL.md". That path exists in one hosted environment and nowhere else. On a laptop or another client it is a dead instruction. The skill now asks the user whether they want an Excel file. It creates the file only on a yes, with the file name that the skill defines. Otherwise, it uses tables in the chat. The skill names no path. All 31 skills received the same sentence.

Low · fixed

The skill stated the escalation ages as rules without saying that they are practice

"Deposits in transit older than 10 business days" and "outstanding cheques older than 90 days" are sound bookkeeping convention. However, the skill presents them beside funds-availability and unclaimed-property rules that are published. One clause now follows the two bullets: "these ages are practice; adjust to the entity's bank and state". The clause also says to save the chosen ages as a preference in Step 7. The quoted bullets themselves did not change.
