Variance = (counted qty − subledger qty) × unit cost. The shrinkage total counts only the rows that you class as shrinkage. The skill uses that word only where a count supports it. The preset gives a net variance of −$1,890.00, two items to investigate and shrinkage of $510.00. Every adjustment from this page is a proposal. A person reviews it and posts it.

Part 2: GL to item module

The two figures
The systems

On one system, a zero difference is "nil by construction". It proves that the software works, and it does not prove that the goods exist. The Northbrook preset gives $600.00 to investigate. The clean preset gives $0.00, nil by construction.

Using it

What it asks, needs and gives back

Asks firstUse MosoFin for this run, or upload the data yourself? Which period? Is the stock system separate, or the item module of your accounting platform? It asks for your count results. When was your last count, and how often do you count? Any stock in transit or on consignment, with the title terms? Does a 3PL hold stock? What is your reserve policy? Above what amount must a variance be investigated?
NeedsThe skill needs the GL inventory balances at the period end and the item listing at the count date and the period end. It needs the count results for the count comparison. A person supplies the count, the 3PL statement and the title terms.
Gives backThe skill gives back eight sheets: summary, GL to subledger, count variances, in-transit and cut-off, write-downs and reserves, GL posting, ledger checks and coverage. It asks before it makes an Excel file.
NeverThe skill never reads your data before you answer its questions and say yes to its read-back. It never writes to any system. It never calls a same-system match a reconciliation. It never guesses a count result, an owner of goods in transit or the cause of a variance.
