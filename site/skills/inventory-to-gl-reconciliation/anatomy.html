The structure of the inventory reconciliation skill, and what it does

On main the skill is a 156-line card and seven reference files, 1,267 lines in all. The split commit f077db2 made the card on 25 October 2026. Pull request #1 merged it on 26 October 2026. The bookkeeping-audit commit e9996eb changed the skill on 27 October 2026. The skill first asks if the books and the stock system are one system or two. It asks for the physical count, because only a count tests that the goods exist. It posts nothing. This page shows how the files connect, the run stage by stage, the tools and sources behind each step, and the audit findings.

A9. The roll-forward can subtract a shipment two times

Step 4 rolls the count forward with "receipts − shipments" between the count date and period end. It does not say what to do with a shipment that left before the count but went into the books after it. That shipment must not come off again. The worked example shows the risk: 40 units of A-120 left on 25 September and went into the books on 28 September.
