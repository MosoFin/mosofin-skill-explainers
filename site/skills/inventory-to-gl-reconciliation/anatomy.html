How the inventory to GL reconciliation is built, and what it does

A 156-line card with seven reference files behind it, split on 25 Oct 2026 from a 1,017-line SKILL.md, then corrected on 27 Oct after a senior-bookkeeper audit found that it read item quantities as they stand today rather than at the count date, and labelled a same-system difference nil without computing it. The skill compares the general ledger, the inventory subledger and the physical count, classifies each variance, lists negative quantities, tests cut-off, ties the reserve and trends shrinkage, starting from one question: are the subledger and the GL two systems, or one? This page shows where the weight sits, the run step by step, what the audit found and how each finding was fixed.

Seven findings, all fixed on 27 Oct, with the standard behind each

An independent senior-bookkeeper audit on 27 Oct 2026 gave the verdict fit with fixes. Each finding was checked against the skill before acting; all seven were fixed in e9996eb, none partly, none rejected.

High · fixed

H1 — Subledger quantities were not taken at the count date or the period end

Step 0 read search_items for “quantity on hand”, which is the quantity at the moment of the read. A run in October compared a 26 September count with October quantities, and nothing rolled the count forward to the period end.

Fixed in e9996eb. Step 0 reads book quantities and values as at the count date and the period end; if only current quantity is available, the subledger leg is [manual] and the user is asked for inventory valuation exports at both dates. Step 4 rolls the count forward (count + receipts − shipments) and lists the movements. The upload row asks for the listing as of both dates.

Behind it · standard physical count practice: compare at the count date, then roll forward references/method.md Steps 0, 4 · SKILL.md upload checklist
High · fixed

H2 — “Nil by construction” hid the commonest real GL-to-subledger differences

The card said a same-system leg is “reported as nil by construction”. On QuickBooks Online the inventory asset account can still take journal entries, account-coded bills and opening balances, none of which moves item quantities, so the two regularly disagree.

Fixed in e9996eb. Step 3 and the card's rule now compute the difference even where the systems are one: zero is nil by construction; non-zero is explained by listing the postings that bypass the item module. Step 0b, Step 2, Sheet 2, edge-cases.md and output.md match.

Behind it · QuickBooks Online inventory behaviour; standard reconciliation practice references/method.md Steps 0b, 2, 3 · SKILL.md rules and Stage 4
Medium · fixed

M1 — Cleanup journals would break the item module on same-system platforms

Shrinkage and overage were proposed as journal entries to the inventory account, which change the GL but not item quantities, creating next month's difference.

Fixed in e9996eb. Step 8 proposes count results on an item-module subledger as per-SKU quantity adjustments (SKU, new quantity, adjustment account, count date); journal entries only for a separate-system subledger, the reserve and the NRV write-down.

Behind it · platform practice for tracked inventory (quantity adjustments post their own entry) references/method.md Step 8
Medium · fixed

M2 — Overage was credited to Other Income

The overage entry read “CR Inventory Adjustments / Other Income”, moving a count correction below gross margin.

Fixed in e9996eb. Overage now credits Inventory Shrinkage / Adjustments within cost of sales, the same account the shrinkage entry debits.

Behind it · standard practice: net count adjustments within cost of sales references/method.md Step 8
Medium · fixed

M3 — No investigation threshold was asked

Step 6 investigates each “material” variance and Step 10 saves a materiality threshold, but intake never collected one.

Fixed in e9996eb. New intake question I asks the threshold per SKU and in total; without it, all variances are ranked by value and the output says no threshold was applied.

Behind it · standard materiality practice in reconciliations SKILL.md intake I · references/method.md Step 6
