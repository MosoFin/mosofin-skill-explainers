From a trial balance to statements that tie

This page shows how the skill turns a trial balance into five financial statements, one level at a time. It starts with 21 account balances. It ends with seven tie-outs, each with its difference, and a package that the owner can send to a bank. The page quotes every rule from the skill and works every number in front of you.

How it thinks

Twelve rules the whole skill runs on

Every level below applies one or more of these rules. The examples use one made-up company, Northbrook Trading. It is a wholesale trading company with share capital, and it reports in US dollars under IFRS. The year ends on Tuesday 30 June 2026, and the skill runs on Wednesday 15 July 2026. All figures are in thousands of US dollars ($000).

Part 2: Split the loan

The loan
The rest of the balance sheet

The current portion comes from the amortization schedule, never from the ledger. The ratio of current assets to current liabilities is an illustration on this page. The skill computes no ratios. The Northbrook preset gives a current portion of 40 and a non-current portion of 80. Current liabilities go from 104 to 144, and the ratio goes from 3.40 to 2.46.

Using it

What it asks, needs and gives back

Asks firstA: use Mosofin for this run, or upload the data yourself? B: which framework (US GAAP, IFRS, a local GAAP or a special-purpose basis), and what legal form? C: which period, and which columns? D: for a group, consolidated or separate statements? E: please give the prior-year statements as issued. F: how do you set the current portion of debt and leases? G: whole units, thousands or millions? H: income statement by function or by nature? I: anything special, such as discontinued operations, segments, another presentation currency or EPS?
NeedsThe skill needs the trial balance at the current period end, with account types. It also needs the trial balance at each comparative period end, on the same basis. For cash flows and equity, it needs the balance sheet at the start of the period. It needs the GL for the fixed asset, loan and equity accounts as well. You give the schedules and the prior-year statements.
Gives backThe skill gives back five statements with comparatives, all seven tie-outs with their differences, the reclassifications and a coverage sheet. These fill a workpaper of up to nine sheets. It asks before it makes the Excel file or a PDF.
NeverThe skill never reads your data before you answer its questions and say yes to its read-back. It never writes to any system. It never defaults a framework, a split, a rounding basis or a share count. It never adds entity packages together, and it writes no notes.
