The structure of the financial statement builder skill, and what it does

The skill was one 1,083-line file. On main it is now a 161-line card and seven reference files, 1,340 lines in total. Pull request #1 merged them on 26 October 2026. A bookkeeping-audit commit, 4d143f4, then changed the card and the method on 27 October 2026. Two things set this skill apart. It never takes the platform's own balance sheet as a finished statement, because that report always balances. And it reports seven tie-outs, each with its difference, and posts nothing. This page shows how the files connect, the run stage by stage, and the source behind each step. It also shows the audit: three findings fixed on main, one check passed, seven open findings and three open observations.

A5. Tie-out 5 does not name a dividends account

Tie-out 5 compares closing retained earnings with the RE line plus current-year net income on the balance sheet. It does not name a dividends or distributions account that the books keep apart before closing, as QuickBooks often does. It also does not name owner's capital for an LLC or a partnership, although Step 2 gives that equity form. The worked example maps Dividends paid to the RE line, so the tie works: 204 + 38 = 242.

A7. Nothing labels the package as unaudited

The footer from Step 9 names the entity, period, currency, rounding, source and mock status. It names no level of assurance. [6] A CPA can compile, review or audit statements, and each gives a different level of assurance. An owner who gives the package to a bank may want a label such as "prepared by management, unaudited". This is an observation. No source makes it a rule.
