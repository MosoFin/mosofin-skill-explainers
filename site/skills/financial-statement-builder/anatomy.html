How the financial statement builder is built, and what it does

A 161-line card with seven reference files behind it, split on 25 Oct 2026 from a 1,083-line SKILL.md, then corrected on 27 Oct after a senior-bookkeeper audit found that two of the five statements it promised, cash flows and changes in equity, had no procedure at all. The skill turns a trial balance into a Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Comprehensive Income, Cash Flows and Changes in Equity in the chosen framework, then proves seven cross-statement tie-outs on the statements it built, each with its difference. This page shows where the weight sits, the run step by step, what the audit found and how each finding was fixed.

Nine findings, all fixed on 27 Oct, with the standard behind each

An independent senior-bookkeeper audit on 27 Oct 2026 gave the verdict fit with fixes. Each finding was checked against the skill before acting; all nine were fixed in 4d143f4, none partly, none rejected.

High · fixed

H1 — Cash flows and changes in equity had no procedure and no inputs

Steps 6 and 7 said only “Build it by the indirect method, which is a full procedure of its own” and “Prepare it as a procedure of its own”, after the hand-offs to two unshipped skills were removed. Four of the seven tie-outs depend on these statements, and neither path asked for the opening balance sheet or the movements they need.

Fixed in 4d143f4. Step 6 is written out: operating cash from net income, non-cash items and each working-capital change; investing and financing shown gross from the GL; the net change must equal the change in cash with no plug; non-cash transactions listed separately. Step 7 rolls each equity component forward. Both intake paths now ask for the start-of-period balance sheet and GL detail for fixed asset, loan and equity accounts.

Behind it · ASC 230 / IAS 7 (investing and financing flows shown gross) references/method.md Steps 0, 6, 7 · SKILL.md upload checklist
High · fixed

H2 — Tie-out 3 compared net income plus OCI with net income

It read “Net Income + OCI on Statement of CI = Net Income on Statement of Equity Changes”. Net income plus OCI is total comprehensive income, so the check failed by the OCI amount on every correct package that had OCI.

Fixed in 4d143f4. Tie-out 3 now reads: net income on the income statement = net income in the retained-earnings column of the equity statement; OCI on the statement of comprehensive income = the movement in AOCI.

Behind it · ASC 220 / IAS 1.106 (statement of changes in equity) references/method.md Step 8
Medium · fixed

M1 — A month-end TB gives year to date, and the balance sheet had no current-year earnings line

Intake asked only “Which period end”, so “August financials” would come out as a year-to-date P&L. On a pre-closing TB the year's profit sits outside retained earnings, and tie-out 5 compared against the wrong line.

Fixed in 4d143f4. Intake C asks the period length and the columns (month, YTD, prior year, budget); Step 1 reads the month's P&L or differences the prior month's TB; the equity template gains a current-year net income line; tie-out 5 compares against retained earnings plus current-year net income.

Behind it · standard month-end reporting practice; the platforms show retained earnings and current-year net income as separate lines until year end SKILL.md intake C · references/method.md Steps 1, 2, 8
Medium · fixed

M2 — Special-purpose frameworks were missing from the choice

Intake offered only “US GAAP, IFRS or a local GAAP”. Most small-business statements for a bank are on the income tax basis, cash or modified cash, or FRF for SMEs, which have no OCI statement and title each statement with the basis.

Fixed in 4d143f4. Intake B now offers the special-purpose bases and asks the legal form; Steps 5 and 6 say comprehensive income and cash flows are not required under a special-purpose framework unless asked, and each statement is titled with its basis.

Behind it · AICPA special-purpose frameworks and FRF for SMEs; statements compiled under SSARS must describe their basis SKILL.md intake B · references/method.md Steps 5, 6
Medium · fixed

M3 — Tie-out 4 could not fail

“Cash at end of period on Cash Flow Statement = Cash on Balance Sheet” always passes if closing cash is copied from the balance sheet or the operating section absorbs the residual.

Fixed in 4d143f4. Tie-out 4 now reads: opening cash plus the three computed sections, none plugged, equals closing cash on the balance sheet, with the same definition of cash on both, restricted cash included where held.

Behind it · ASC 230 as amended by ASU 2016-18 (restricted cash in the cash flow statement) references/method.md Step 8
Medium · fixed

M4 — No check for suspense, uncategorised or setup accounts

Neither the TB validation nor the quality checks looked for balances in Uncategorized, Ask My Accountant, suspense, Undeposited Funds or Opening Balance Equity, so the mapping step would have put them in an “Other” line.

Fixed in 4d143f4. Step 1 lists every non-zero clearing, suspense or setup balance as an exception before mapping, asks the user how to treat each, and never buries one in “Other”.

Behind it · standard bookkeeping review practice before statements are issued references/method.md Step 1
