The structure of the payroll reconciliation skill, and what it does

The skill was one 909-line file. On main it is now a 159-line card and seven reference files. Pull request #1 merged them on 26 October 2026. A bookkeeping-audit commit, 8c9a1b1, then changed the method on 27 October 2026. Two rules set this skill apart. It never puts a gross-based check in place of the wage-base check. And it never reports a tie against a source that it did not get. This page shows how the files connect, the run stage by stage, and the source behind each step. It also shows the audit findings: four changes on main, one check that main passes, seven open findings and three open observations.

The audit checked the seven sources on 28 Oct 2026 through search results. Nobody opened the pages. Six sources are US and one is UK. The skill is jurisdiction-agnostic, and other countries have their own rules. Some items have no outside authority behind them. They are the four-source frame, the six bridge causes, the eight sheets and the [auto], [gated] and [manual] verdicts. They also include the NOT PERFORMED rule, the order by consequence and every example figure. The worked example on the public pages uses made-up figures, and its tax amounts come from a provider report, not from a rate. The public pages present these items as the skill's rules or as the example's choices. Ask your accountant or payroll adviser what applies to you.
