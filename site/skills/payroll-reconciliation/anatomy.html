How the payroll reconciliation is built, and what it does

A 159-line card with seven reference files behind it, split on 25 Oct 2026 from a 909-line SKILL.md, then corrected on 27 Oct after a senior-bookkeeper audit found that Step 4 ran the very gross-for-taxable substitute the card forbids and explained the gap away. The skill ties payroll's four sources of truth to each other: the register to the GL, net pay to the bank, each withholding and employer liability rolled forward, the quarter- and year-end filings to the books, and each tax to its taxable wage base. This page shows where the weight sits, the run step by step, what the audit found and how each finding was fixed.

Seven findings, all fixed on 27 Oct, with the standard behind each

An independent senior-bookkeeper audit on 27 Oct 2026 gave the verdict fit with fixes. Each finding was checked against the skill before acting; all seven were fixed in 8c9a1b1, none partly, none rejected.

High · fixed

H1 — Step 4 substituted gross for taxable wages and explained the difference away

“Where the register is absent, this comparison runs on gross and will differ by exactly the pre-tax deductions and capped amounts. Say that.” Without the register neither amount is known, and the gap also holds accrual timing, capitalised labour and real filing errors.

Fixed in 8c9a1b1. Without the register the taxable-wages comparison is NOT PERFORMED, as in Step 5. Only tax on the filing is tied to tax credited to the liability accounts, and GL gross to the filing's gross or total-pay line where the form has one; any gross-to-taxable gap is reported as “unexplained, needs register”.

Behind it · the skill's own rule against a gross-based substitute; the filings it ties to (US Form 941, UK RTI) report taxable wages references/method.md Step 4
Medium · fixed

M1 — The register-to-GL and filing ties ignored cash versus accrual

Registers, filings and wage statements are on a pay-date basis; GL expense is earned, with the month-end accrual and its reversal. Step 1's causes table had no line for that, and Step 0 read only “wages and employer cost expense”.

Fixed in 8c9a1b1. Step 1's causes table opens with accrued wages (the accrued-payroll movement); Step 0 reads every account that receives payroll, confirmed with the user; wage statements agree to GL through a bridge (accrual movement, capitalised labour, pre-tax deductions, imputed income).

Behind it · accrual versus pay-date basis; year-end wage statements (W-2, P60, T4) on a pay-date basis references/method.md Steps 0, 1, 4 · references/edge-cases.md · SKILL.md Mosofin path
Medium · fixed

M2 — “Negative liability balances are always wrong” over-claimed

Benefits billed in advance, a deposit made before the journal is posted, or an over-remittance the authority will credit all give legitimate debit balances; calling them errors drives a correction that reverses a real asset.

Fixed in 8c9a1b1. Every location now says a negative liability is always an exception — a posting error, an over-remittance to claim back or a prepayment to reclassify — never left in a liability account at a reporting date.

Behind it · ASC 210-20 / IAS 32.42 (no netting of an asset into a liability) references/method.md Step 3 · overview.md · output.md · tools-and-glossary.md · SKILL.md
Medium · fixed

M3 — On the connected path without a payroll platform, the register was not asked for

Intake D said only that steps would be partial, and the Mosofin path's “still ask for” list left the register out.

Fixed in 8c9a1b1. Intake D asks the user to upload the register when no payroll platform is connected, and the Mosofin-path bullet includes it.

Behind it · standard practice: reconcile to the independent source when it can be had SKILL.md intake D and Mosofin path
Medium · fixed

M4 — Per-employee caps conflicted with “never employee by employee”

Step 5 says caps apply per employee; the card said the skill never works per employee, so the cap test would be skipped or the personal-data rule broken.

Fixed in 8c9a1b1. Step 5 does per-employee arithmetic in the session only, reports only aggregates and counts, and refers to an exception by reference number; the card's rule now says it reports at account and total level.

Behind it · per-employee wage base caps (for example the US Social Security wage base); data minimisation for personal data references/method.md Step 5 · SKILL.md rules
