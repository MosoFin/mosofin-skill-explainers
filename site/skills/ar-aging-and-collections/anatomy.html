The structure of the AR aging and collections skill, and what it does

The skill was one 845-line file. On main it is now a 154-line card and seven reference files. Pull request #1 merged them on 26 October 2026. A bookkeeping-audit commit, 4e8a3ac, then changed the method on 27 October 2026. Two things set this skill apart. It never takes an invoice search as the full list of unpaid invoices. And it contacts no customer: every reminder is a draft that a person reviews and sends. This page shows how the files connect, the run stage by stage, and the source behind each step. It also shows the audit findings: three fixed on main, one check that main passes, two open findings and five open observations.

The audit checked the five sources on 28 Oct 2026 through search results. Nobody opened the pages. All five sources are US. Other countries have their own rules on bankruptcy and bad debts. Some items have no outside authority behind them. They are the six buckets, the action bands, the 0.01 tolerance, the seven sheets, the draft marking and every example figure. The worked example uses made-up figures. The public pages present these as the skill's rules or as the example's choices. Ask your accountant what applies to you.

Observation · open

Intake question F does not name an insolvency notice

Step 5 and validation scenario 7 stop ordinary reminders after a bankruptcy or insolvency notice. But question F asks only for disputes, credit holds, tiers and payment history.
