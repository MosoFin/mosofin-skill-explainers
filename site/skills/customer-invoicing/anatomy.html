How the customer invoicing skill is built, and what it does

A 159-line card with seven reference files behind it, split on 25 Oct 2026 from an 899-line file. It prepares customer invoices that carry everything a valid tax invoice needs, runs the same rules over invoices already issued with a duplicate-number and sequence-gap check, and proposes the matching AR / Revenue / Tax entry. It never creates, numbers or sends an invoice. This page shows where the weight sits, the run step by step, what the 27 Oct bookkeeping audit found and how each finding was fixed.

Audit → findings

Eight findings, all fixed on 27 Oct, with the standard behind each

An independent senior-bookkeeper audit read the card and every reference file; each finding was checked against the skill before acting. Severity is the auditor's.

High · fixed

The proposed entry did not match the totals, so a discounted invoice would not balance

Step 3 computed a discount and a shipping line, but the Step 4 entry was “DR AR $total inclusive / CR Revenue $subtotal / CR Output Tax $tax / CR Other”, with no discount line; and “Subtotal” was not defined, so shipping could be credited twice.

Fix. Subtotal is defined as goods and service lines before discount, excluding shipping. The entry gains DR Sales Discounts (or netted against revenue, stated which) and must foot: DR AR = subtotal − discount + shipping + tax, checked against the amount due before it is presented; validate mode compares it to the invoice's own total.

Behind it: double-entry bookkeeping: a proposed entry must balance; a sales discount is recorded as contra-revenue or netted, one method consistently.

Medium · fixed

Delivery was never asked, so revenue could be booked before it was earned

Intake E asked only for the invoice date and tax point, and the deferred form of the entry was used only if the user volunteered it. Invoicing in advance (deposits, annual plans, milestones) is common.

Fix. Intake E asks for the delivery date or service period per line; Step 4 credits Deferred Revenue for any portion delivered after the invoice date, and validate mode flags such invoices mapped to income.

Behind it: ASC 606-10-25-23 and IFRS 15.31: revenue is recognised when control transfers, not when the invoice is raised.

SKILL.md intake E · references/method.md Step 4
Medium · fixed

The tax formula was wrong for tax-inclusive prices, and rounding was not set

The only formula was “subtotal × tax rate = line tax”, which overstates tax on a tax-inclusive price, and validate mode reported “rounding differences” with no rule to test against.

Fix. Step 2 adds tax = gross × rate ÷ (1 + rate) for tax-inclusive prices and rounds per the entity's stated method; validate mode recomputes on that method and reports any residual over 0.01. Intake G asks whether prices include tax and how tax is rounded.

Behind it: VAT/GST practice: the tax fraction of a tax-inclusive price is rate ÷ (1 + rate), and line-level or total-level rounding is a stated choice.

Medium · fixed

The gap and duplicate check had no defined population

It searched “recent periods” without fixing a range, did not separate deleted invoices (absent from a search) from voided ones (present at zero), and on uploads could run on a handful of PDFs.

Fix. Step 0 fixes the range, pulls every invoice in it including voids (a paged search, or an upload list with voids), reports gaps and voids separately, and says deletions can only be confirmed from the audit log, which the skill cannot read. The upload row requires the range list in validate mode.

Behind it: standard sequential-numbering audit practice: gaps are tested over a complete, defined range.

Low · fixed

Four low findings, all fixed

  • Gate 3 verdicts contradicted method.md. Line items now read “[manual] what to bill; [auto] descriptions, prices, tax codes”, and customer PO “[auto] from prior invoices”.
  • “Sales discount” was defined two ways; the glossary now allows contra-revenue or netting, one method used consistently.
  • Foreign-currency invoices showed one amount; Step 4 now shows invoice currency, rate and functional amount per line (ASC 830-20-30-1 and IAS 21.21), and Step 1 adds local-currency VAT/GST as a [manual] field where required, as under EU VAT Directive 2006/112 article 230.
  • VAT-only language read as universal; Step 1 notes that US-style sales tax has no reclaim or tax point, and confirms the destination address and exemption certificates instead.
