The structure of the invoice data extractor skill, and what it does

The skill was one 970-line file. On main it is now a 150-line card and seven reference files, with 1,200 lines in all. Pull request #1 merged the card format on 26 October 2026. A bookkeeping-audit commit, 2850d13, then changed the card, the method and the edge cases on 27 October 2026. The skill reads supplier invoices into fields and checks each invoice against the books. It posts nothing. This page shows how the files connect, the run stage by stage, the evidence tools by step and the source behind each step. It also gives the audit for the skill owner: one check that passed and nine open findings.

Some items have no outside authority behind them. They are the Clean, Review and Stop statuses, the confidence levels, the six sheets and the file name. The public pages present these as the skill's rules or as the example's choices. Ask your accountant what applies to you.

A3. The audit fix left old formulas behind

Commit 2850d13 made amounts "as printed" and added a tax-inclusive rule. In that rule, the lines sum to Total, and Subtotal is Total minus Tax. But Step 3 still defines Line total as "Subtotal + tax", a computed figure. Step 4 still defines Subtotal (pre-tax) as "Sum of line subtotals" and Balance due as "Total − paid". For a tax-inclusive invoice, the old Subtotal definition contradicts the new rule.
