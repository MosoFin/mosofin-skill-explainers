How the invoice data extractor is built, and what it does

A 150-line card with seven reference files behind it, split on 25 Oct 2026 from a 970-line SKILL.md, the longest original of this group. The skill turns a vendor invoice — PDF, scan or photo — into fields a system can use, never guessing a field it cannot see, then checks each invoice against the books before anyone pays it: duplicate number, already paid, Bill-To, vendor, terms, PO. This page shows where the weight sits, the run step by step, and what the 27 Oct audit found and how each finding was handled.

Audit → findings

Eight findings: seven fixed, one partly fixed on 27 Oct

An independent senior-bookkeeper review of the card and every reference file. Verdict: fit with fixes. The finding that mattered most was that the duplicate and already-paid checks had no defined look-back.

High · fixed

H1 — The duplicate and already-paid checks could run against a population with no paid bills in it

The upload row asked for "an AP listing or aged payables". Aged payables lists open bills only, so a re-submitted invoice already paid last quarter passed both checks and came back Clean. On the Mosofin path the bill search had no date range, and payments made outside the bill process were not read.

Fix. Step 0 defines the look-back: by vendor, open and paid bills, payments and direct purchases, over a window the user agrees (or the saved tolerance), stated on the Validation Results row; a match is the same normalized invoice number, or the same amount within that window. The upload row is now "vendor transaction history", and aged payables alone is not accepted. No fixed 18-month or 30-day figure, because the house brief forbids hard-coded windows.

Basis: standard AP practice: check a new invoice against the vendor's full billing and payment history, open and paid.

method.md Step 0 · SKILL.md upload checklist
High · fixed

H2 — Tax-inclusive invoices failed the cross-check or had their tax counted twice

"Subtotal (pre-tax) = sum of line subtotals" picks up gross line amounts on a tax-inclusive invoice; adding the tax on top broke the cross-check by exactly the tax, or left a pre-tax column overstated by the recoverable tax.

Fix. Step 4 gains a tax-inclusive rule: lines sum to Total; Subtotal = Total − the tax printed on the invoice; two cross-checks; if no tax amount is printed, Subtotal and Tax stay null and are flagged. Tax is never calculated from a rate.

Basis: VAT and GST rules: input tax on a valid tax invoice is recorded apart from the net cost.

method.md Step 4 · SKILL.md Stage 3
Medium · fixed

M1 — "Never guesses a field" was broken by quantity defaults and calculated amounts

Quantity was "default 1 if not shown", the line subtotal was "Quantity × Unit price", and the due date could be "calculated from terms" without saying so — values not on the document, hiding a line where the printed amount differs.

Fix. Quantity and unit price as shown, else null; the line amount as printed, with any qty × price ≠ amount flagged; the due date as printed, and a date derived from terms goes in a separate due_date_calculated field.

Basis: the skill's own output standard that every figure traces to the source document by file and page.

method.md Steps 2–3 · JSON
Medium · fixed

M2 — "A row I can post", but no account or tax code in the output

Neither sheet had a column for account, tax code or the period the cost belongs to, though the vendor's default account and coding history are already read; and nothing flagged a service period outside the invoice month.

Fix. Sheet 2 gains Tax Code (as printed) and Suggested Account (with its source), marked as suggestions; Review gains "service period not within the invoice month — a prepaid or accrual decision is needed".

Basis: accrual-basis cut-off: a cost booked in the wrong period is a cut-off error (prepaid and accrued expense practice, ASC 340).

method.md Sheet 2, Step 6
Medium · fixed

M3 — The PO check ran, but its result never reached a status

An invoice citing a PO missing from the books, closed, or for another vendor could still be Clean; the result only appeared on Sheet 5.

Fix. Clean now requires a cited PO to be found and open; Review covers a PO not found, closed, for a different vendor, or exceeded by the invoice total, pointing to three-way-match for line-level matching.

Basis: standard purchase-to-pay control: an invoice citing a PO is checked against that PO before payment.

Three low findings: two fixed, one partly fixed

  • L1 · Fields that drive a Stop missing from the main sheet. Sheet 1 and the JSON gain Bill-To, Remit-to (masked), Amount Paid and Balance Due, so a reviewer can see what triggered a "Stop — Bill-To is a different entity". Fixed.
  • L2 · Credit memos checked against bills, not vendor credits. A credit memo is now checked against posted vendor credits, with whether it is already applied. Partly fixed: the audit's tool name search_vendor_credits was not written in, because this skill does not name it; the read is resolved from the Gate 2 catalogue.
  • L3 · "Reads nothing until Stage 1" beside "extraction may start before the gates". The card rule now reads "Reads nothing from your books until Stage 1 is answered", so reading the user's own document is not a breach. Fixed.
