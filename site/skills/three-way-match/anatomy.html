How the three-way match is built, and what it does

Was one 729-line file; now a 137-line card and six reference files, every line of the original kept. Its distinctive move is that it names no evidence tools at all: Gate 2 asks three capability questions of the live catalogue — are purchase orders exposed, are item receipts, is approval status — and the answers decide which of fifteen exception tests can run. This page shows where the weight sits, the run step by step, what the audit found, and how those three questions come out against a real workspace.
