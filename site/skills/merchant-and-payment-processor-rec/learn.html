Check fees against the published price

Published US rates as listed by Stripe1, Square5 and PayPal7, checked 20 October 2026. Your contract may differ — international cards, currency conversion and other plans cost more. The skill says "revenue ≈ net payouts" and "a near-empty fee account" without fixing a number. This page treats within 1% as "≈" and under 10% of the actual fees as "near-empty" — your own judgement may differ.

Sources

Where every fact on this page comes from

Rules are the skill's own. Facts below come from the payment apps' own documentation and from regulators and standard-setters. Rates and timings are as published when checked on 20 October 2026 — follow the link for today's figure.

    Not backed by an outside authority: keeping a separate clearing account for each payment app is bookkeeping practice that the skill requires, not a published standard. The page marks it as such where it appears.

    Using it

    What it asks, needs and gives back

    Asks firstMosofin or upload? Which payment app? Which month? Each sale or one entry per payout? Any money held back?
    NeedsYour books — connected or exported — the payment app's payout report, and the bank statement.
    Gives backDoes it match, what's off and why, suggested corrections, and where every number came from.
    NeverChanges your books, moves money, or guesses a number. It reads nothing before you say yes.
