The structure of the payroll journal entry builder skill, and what it does

The skill was one 917-line file. On main it is now a 161-line card and seven reference files, 1,156 lines in all. The split commit d4726ff made the card on 25 October 2026, and pull request #1 merged it on 26 October 2026. The bookkeeping-audit commit 76d1e74 changed the method on 27 October 2026. The skill builds proposed payroll entries before a person posts them. It keeps the employer entry apart from the employee entry and ties each line to the register. This page shows how the files connect, the run stage by stage, the tools and sources behind each step, and the audit.

The audit checked the five sources on 28 Oct 2026 through search results. Nobody opened the pages. All five are US sources. The skill is jurisdiction-agnostic, and other countries have their own rules. Some items have no outside authority behind them. They are the two-entry rule, the line tie, the run-over-run comparison, the ESTIMATE rule, the eight sheets and the verdict tags. The worked example on the public pages uses made-up figures. Its tax amounts come from the register of PayrollCo, the example's provider, and the pages apply no rate. Taxable wages can differ from gross pay, for example after pre-tax benefit deductions. Ask your accountant or payroll adviser what applies to you.
