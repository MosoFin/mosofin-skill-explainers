How the payroll journal entry builder is built, and what it does

A 161-line card with seven reference files behind it, split on 25 Oct 2026 from a 917-line SKILL.md, then corrected on 27 Oct after a senior-bookkeeper audit found that its headline control, gross = net + withholdings, holds on any balanced entry and so could never catch the error it was sold for. The skill turns a payroll register into the entries that record the run: gross pay, withholdings, net pay, the employer's own costs kept in a separate entry, department allocation, capitalised labour, net pay funding and the period-end accrual. This page shows where the weight sits, the run step by step, what the audit found and how each finding was fixed.

Eight findings, all fixed on 27 Oct, with the standard behind each

An independent senior-bookkeeper audit on 27 Oct 2026 gave the verdict fit with fixes. Each finding was checked against the skill before acting; all eight were fixed in 76d1e74, none partly, none rejected.

High · fixed

H1 — The “mixing error” test could not fail on a balanced entry

Step 7 claimed that an employer contribution credited to an employee withholding account breaks “gross = net + withholdings”. It does not: once the entry balances the identity holds by construction. The audit's worked case, with employer NI pushed into the employee entry, passes both that identity and “total expense = gross + employer costs”.

Fixed in 76d1e74. The starred check is now: each line ties to the register — gross debited = register gross, each withholding = its register column, net = register net, each employer charge = its column. Gross = net + withholdings stays only as a check on the register itself. The card, edge-cases.md, output.md and Sheet 2 match.

Behind it · standard practice: agree every payroll journal line to the register's column totals references/method.md Step 7 · SKILL.md description, rules, Stage 5
High · fixed

H2 — Benefits in kind were booked as extra wage expense

The edge case said taxable non-cash benefits “increase the expense … without a cash net-pay effect”. The benefit's cost was already expensed when bought, so debiting the imputed value to wages books it twice, and the extra tax withheld does reduce net pay.

Fixed in 76d1e74. The edge case now says the imputed value is not debited to wages again; the extra withholding (reducing net pay) and any employer tax on the benefit are booked; gross is tied to the register excluding imputed income or with an equal non-cash offset. The glossary line is corrected.

Behind it · IRS Publication 15-B (fringe benefits as taxable wages for withholding); UK Class 1A NIC as the employer's tax on benefits references/edge-cases.md · references/tools-and-glossary.md
Medium · fixed

M1 — The period-end accrual needs an estimate the card forbade

Step 6 needed the register for the straddling run, which usually does not exist at close, while the card said never estimate. The trigger also missed a pay period that ends before the period end but is paid after it.

Fixed in 76d1e74. The trigger is now any wages earned on or before the period end and paid after it. If that run is not processed, the accrual is estimated from the last complete run per working day, labelled “ESTIMATE — from <run>”, and trued up through the reversal. The card names it as the one permitted estimate.

Behind it · ASC 710-10-25 / IAS 19.11 (wages expensed when the service is rendered) references/method.md Step 6 · SKILL.md rules
Medium · fixed

M2 — The accounting basis was assumed readable

Gate 3 marked “cash or accrual” as “Now [auto] — Readable from the reads”, and the upload path never asked; a cash-basis business would be handed an accrual it should not post.

Fixed in 76d1e74. Intake C and the upload row ask cash or accrual; Gate 3 marks it Required, ask, not readable; Step 6 applies only on an accrual basis.

Behind it · cash versus accrual basis of accounting SKILL.md intake C · references/gates.md Gate 3 · references/method.md Step 6
Medium · fixed

M3 — Prior runs and headcount could come from the wrong objects

Prior runs came from search_journal_entries, but platform payroll posts as paycheck and tax-payment transactions, not journal entries; headcount came from search_employees, which is often stale and is not the number paid.

Fixed in 76d1e74. Step 0 reads prior runs from get_general_ledger on the wage, liability and clearing accounts, uses search_journal_entries only for the template, and takes headcount as the count of employees paid from the register, with search_employees as a cross-check.

Behind it · QuickBooks Online Payroll transaction types references/method.md Step 0 · references/tools-and-glossary.md
