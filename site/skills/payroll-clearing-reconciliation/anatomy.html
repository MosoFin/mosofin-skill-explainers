The structure of the payroll clearing reconciliation skill, and what it does

The skill was one 1,001-line file. On main it is now a 165-line card and seven reference files. Pull request #1 merged them on 26 October 2026. A bookkeeping-audit commit, cbfbd99, then changed the method on 27 October 2026. Two rules set this skill apart. It ages every balance before it explains this month's movement. And it never writes off pay that the business owes to a person. This page shows how the files connect, the run stage by stage, and the source behind each step. It also shows the audit: four changes on main, one check that main passes, five open findings and five open observations.

The audit checked the six sources on 28 Oct 2026 through search results. Nobody opened the pages. Five sources are US, and two of them are for one state only. One source is UK. The skill is jurisdiction-agnostic, and other countries have their own rules. Some items have no outside authority behind them. They are the ageing bands, the 60-day threshold and the "one cycle plus its due-date lag" rule. They also include the lowest-running-balance residual, the nine causes, the [auto], [gated] and [manual] verdicts, the workpaper tabs and every example figure. The worked example on the public pages uses made-up figures, and its tax amounts come from a provider report, not from a rate. It applies no escheatment period to the example company. Ask your accountant, payroll adviser or your state's unclaimed property office what applies to you.

A7. Only the behaviour test finds an unpaid month

In the worked example, the federal account 2210 carries 27,000 at 30 September. 12,600 of it is August, and nobody paid it. The residual does not show it, because each earlier trough was nil. The YTD test ties. Step 7 does not show it within about its first 45 days. Only the behaviour test, "peaks at one period's withholdings", catches it. The card has no due-date check of its own. payroll-reconciliation has that check.

A8. The residual finds only what never cleared

The lowest running balance of a clearing account shows only what stayed for the whole window. In the worked example that is 23.40. The other 4,000.00 came in during the window, so the ageing and Step 7 find it. The card calls the residual "the finding" and "the headline finding". It does say to report all three tests.

A9. No step proves that a small residual is nobody's pay
