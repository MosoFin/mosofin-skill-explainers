The structure of the 13-week cash flow forecast, and what it does

On main the skill is one 937-line file. The rewrite described here turns it into a 138-line card and seven reference files, and it keeps the original text. Two things set this skill apart. It times each receipt by the customer's measured days-to-pay, not by the due date. And the lines that breach a floor never come from the books, so the skill always asks for them. Those lines are payroll, debt service, tax dates, the floor and the facility. This page shows how the files connect, the run step by step, and the source behind each step. It also shows the two audit findings, one fixed in the rewrite and one open.
