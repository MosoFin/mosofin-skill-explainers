A6. No rule says how to count a check that does not apply

The sample line in method Step 6 says "13 of 15 run" and marks the loan split n/a. So it counts a check that does not apply as not run. No rule states this. The worked example follows the sample. For J1 it gives 11 of 15 run and 4 not applicable. Over the batch it gives 52 run, 49 passed, 3 flagged and 23 not applicable.

Low · open

A7. Revenue is called a control account

Step 6 and the edge cases name "revenue, cash, AR or AP" as control accounts. The glossary defines a control account as "a summary account fed by a subledger, like AR or AP". Revenue is a red-flag target, but it is not a control account. The worked example (J2) shows the problem. The pages say "manual entry to revenue", not "revenue control account".
