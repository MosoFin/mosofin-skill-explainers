The structure of the credit card reconciliation, and what it does

The card format is on main of MosoFin/mosofin-finance-skills. It merged in PR #1 on 26 October 2026, and a bookkeeping-audit fix followed on 27 October 2026. The skill was one 973-line file, the largest of the reconciliation skills. On main it is a 144-line card and seven reference files, 1,218 lines in all. What sets this skill apart is a richer book side: the books hold one posting for each card charge. So the skill can test duplicates, recurring subscriptions and capital purchases every month from the books alone, statement or not. This page shows how the files connect, the run step by step, and the source behind each step. It also shows what the audits found, what main fixed, and what this page fixed on the earlier version.

High · fixed on main

The statement-to-books bridge had the signs the wrong way round

An independent bookkeeping audit found this after the split. The old bridge added charges that were in the books but not on the statement. It subtracted charges that were on the statement but not in the books. The card liability is a credit balance, so both signs were wrong. Option B also credited the statement closing balance. That counts the opening balance twice or expenses the payments. The fix reverses the signs, adds a worked example, and makes Option B credit new charges less credits. It also reads the books at the statement closing date and matches statement lines before the bridge. It proves the statement's own arithmetic and checks last month's open items. The same fix adds a personal-charge reclass entry and gross and net labels on Option A. It widens the duplicate window past one day, rewrites the owner-funds edge case, and records cardholders by role in Gate 3. The commit counts 10 findings fixed and 0 rejected. The plain and learn pages followed the old signs until 28 October 2026. They now follow main.
