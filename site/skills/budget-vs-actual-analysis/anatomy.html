How the budget vs. actual analysis is built, and what it does

A 154-line card with seven reference files behind it, split on 25 Oct 2026 from a 922-line file. It measures the gap between plan and result on every line for the month, quarter and year to date, labels each gap favourable or unfavourable by line type, filters for materiality, works out why the material ones happened — volume, price, mix, rate, FX, timing or a one-off — and drafts commentary for a management meeting. The audit on 27 Oct found that the driver decomposition did not add up to the variance and that nothing checked the month was closed; five findings are fixed and one partly.

Six findings: five fixed, one partly, with the practice behind each

An independent senior-bookkeeper audit on 27 Oct 2026 gave the verdict "fit with fixes": the alignment, F/U and "never invent a cause" discipline is strong, but the driver decomposition was not defined so its parts add up, and nothing confirmed the month was closed. Each finding was checked against the skill before acting; fixes are in f9bdf18.

High · fixed

H1 — The driver decomposition double-counted mix and FX, and never proved it added up

Total revenue variance = Volume + Price + Mix + FX, with volume at each product's own budget price (which already includes mix) and price at actual prices in reporting currency (which already includes FX). Personnel listed headcount + rate + a third "timing" amount. The Sheet 5 waterfall could not bridge without a plug.

Fixed. Volume at the budget average price, mix at budget prices, price at the budget FX rate, FX on restatement; the four parts must sum to the line variance, with any residual shown as "unexplained", never plugged (verified algebraically). Personnel uses average heads; hiring timing is commentary. The same sum check applies to cost decompositions.

Basis: Management-accounting sales-variance analysis as taught by CIMA and ACCA.

Medium · fixed

M1 — Nothing confirmed the period was closed

An open month shows missing accruals and unbooked bills as favourable expense variances, which reverse next month — bookkeeping lag explained as performance.

Fixed. Intake C asks whether the period is closed. If not, or unknown, the report is headed "PRELIMINARY — period not closed" and favourable expense variances without ledger evidence are labelled "possible missing accrual — to be confirmed"; bills and journals dated after period end are listed.

Basis: Standard practice: budget-vs-actual runs after the close is signed off, or is marked preliminary.

Medium · fixed

M2 — Accounting basis, sign convention and budget subdivision were not fixed before comparing

Reports default to the company's basis (often cash) while budgets are almost always accrual; budgets store contra lines as positives; a class-level budget was compared with a company-level P&L.

Fixed. Step 1 pulls actuals on the budget's accounting_method (asked; accrual only on the user's yes), normalises signs with contra lines flagged, and matches the budget's class, department or customer subdivision. The upload row asks for the budget's basis and subdivision.

Basis: Standard reporting practice: compare like with like on one basis and one sign convention.
